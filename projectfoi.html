01 — Overview

A platform
built for
everyone.

Florida Orthopedic Hospital is a healthcare management platform used by receptionists, doctors, nurses, administrators, and finance teams to manage patient care, appointments, treatment records, billing, and hospital operations.

The project focused on modernizing a legacy system that had become difficult to navigate after years of incremental updates, while introducing new workflows to support evolving operational needs.

04 — Approach

Reconstruct
first. Then
redesign.

With limited documentation available, the first priority was understanding how the system actually worked. I conducted stakeholder interviews, analyzed existing workflows, reviewed competitor products, and performed a complete audit of the platform.

To establish clarity, I mapped every major workflow and dependency across departments. This documentation became the foundation for design decisions and development planning throughout the project.

05 — Solution

Role-based
experiences
at scale.

The platform was redesigned around role-based experiences, ensuring users could focus on the information and actions relevant to their responsibilities. The new design simplified navigation, reduced unnecessary actions, and improved visibility across departments.

Core workflows redesigned

Simplified multi-step intake with smart field validation.

Appointment Management

Before → After
Before · Registration

7-screen multi-form flow with inconsistent field labeling and no progress indicator. Average completion time: 14 minutes.

After · Registration

Consolidated 3-step wizard with inline validation, smart defaults, and visible progress. Target: under 5 minutes.

Before · Doctor Dashboard

Generic interface showing all system data regardless of role. Doctors navigated 6+ sections to complete a patient review.

After · Doctor Dashboard

Role-filtered view surfacing today's patients, pending approvals, and quick-action shortcuts. Full review in one screen.

06 — Outcome

Simpler ops.
Faster care.
Real impact.

The redesign streamlined hospital operations by simplifying complex workflows and improving accessibility for different user groups. A documented workflow framework became the single source of truth for all future development.

📋
Simplified patient onboarding
Registration reduced from a 7-screen process to a 3-step wizard, cutting completion time significantly.
📱
Remote doctor approvals
Doctors can now review and approve patient information remotely via mobile, removing care bottlenecks.
👁
Cross-department visibility
Shared workflow visibility replaced departmental silos, improving coordination and reducing gaps.
🔀
Reduced navigation complexity
Redundant actions and duplicate paths eliminated through role-based experience architecture.
📖
System documentation created
A workflow framework built from scratch became the source of truth for all future development planning.
⚙️
Design system shipped
Complete component library with tokens, patterns, and usage guidelines handed off for ongoing development.
